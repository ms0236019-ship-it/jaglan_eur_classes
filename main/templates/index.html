    Frequently asked questions

    Do I need any German to join the A1 batch?

    No. The A1 batch starts from the alphabet and basic greetings, so complete beginners are welcome.

    How does the free 2-day demo work?

    Pick a batch, send your details on WhatsApp, and attend two live classes at no cost. You enrol only if you are happy with the teaching.

    Can you guarantee my visa?

    No. Visa decisions are made only by the embassy or immigration authority. We help you prepare the language requirement and your documents, and we explain the process honestly.

    Do you conduct the Goethe or telc exam?

    No. Exams are held only at authorised test centres. We prepare you for the exam and can guide you through booking a slot.

    Which level should I join, and which countries do you cover?

    Take the free level test on this page, or attend the demo and your trainer will place you. We teach German from A1 to B2. For visa guidance we currently help with Germany, Malta, Poland and France.
